## Companies

Three companies I started. Full case studies at [work.pedrumshokouhi.com](https://work.pedrumshokouhi.com).

| Company | What it was | How it ended |
|---|---|---|
| [Megalo](https://megalo.pedrumshokouhi.com) | Fractional ownership of short-term rental homes in Florida, from under $5,000. I raised the capital, bought the homes and shipped an iOS and Android app. | **Bought out by partner investors.** They bought the whole platform in a deal I negotiated and run their own properties on it. |
| [Nora AI](https://nora.pedrumshokouhi.com) | AI prospecting for wealth managers, built as Apex with two engineers. Version 1 found matching prospects from public signals and wrote the first email. | **Paid pilot, then stopped.** Advisors used it for several months. The banks they work under would not permit it because of risk and liability, so I wound it down. |
| [NestDrop](https://nestdrop.pedrumshokouhi.com) | Same-day licensed daycare, booked in under 60 seconds with the child's profile sent ahead. 122 parent and daycare interviews in New York and Montreal. | **Launched, then pivoted.** It could not scale. The market was over-regulated, and I saw no way to dominate a market of many small centers built city by city. I pivoted to a larger opportunity and carried the research and the booking flow forward. |

## Tools

Four command-line tools: two for AI agents, two for company finance. Each is tested Python with no dependencies outside the standard library.

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| [agentprobe](https://github.com/pedrumshokouhi/agentprobe) | Maps a chat agent you do not control by talking to it. Point it at an endpoint and it sends 63 ordinary prompts, then reports identity, system-prompt shape, refusal boundaries, injection resistance, leakage and cost. |
| [flightrecorder](https://github.com/pedrumshokouhi/flightrecorder) | Sits between an agent and any stdio MCP server. Enforces a policy before each call and records every message to a tamper-evident ledger. Replays and diffs runs. |
| [capstack](https://github.com/pedrumshokouhi/capstack) | Cap tables as code. SAFEs, notes, pools and priced rounds live in a text file. Conversion math matches the YC post-money SAFE. Shows dilution diffs and the exit waterfall. |
| [intrinsic](https://github.com/pedrumshokouhi/intrinsic) | Values any listed US company from its SEC filings. Ten years of audited numbers, a DCF with scenarios, a reverse DCF and multiples. |
